When the city leans on taxis

Taxi demand doesn't follow the commuter rush. It builds all afternoon, peaks in the early evening, and stays high late into the night.

Taxi trips by hour of day, April 2026

Yellow and green taxis, 3.70M cleaned trips. Shaded hours are the late-night period (10pm to 6am).

18% of all taxi trips happen late at night, when subway and bus frequency drops. That share became one of the four signals in the priority model.

Five groups, five different actions

Instead of ranking zones by volume alone, every zone was scored on four signals and matched to the intervention its strongest signal points to.

Zones with at least 1,000 trips. Thresholds are percentiles of the April data, with minimum trip counts so small zones can't top the list on percentages alone.

What I found

87%

Taxi demand is concentrated

Manhattan generated 3.20M of 3.70M taxi pickups, so dense Manhattan zones need kerbside and pickup management more than new transport.

58.6% vs 79.3%

Distance reveals the need

Manhattan's trips are mostly short, a candidate for bike or bus alternatives. Queens' are mostly long and airport-linked, a connectivity question.

36%

Some zones rely on taxis at night

The late-night group's trips were 36% after 10pm, double the city average, flagging areas where night-time public transport deserves a review.

Handled responsibly

Public data can still reveal how people move, and comparisons across transport modes are easy to overclaim. Four controls kept the analysis honest.

Data minimisation

Only the fields needed for time, place, distance and cost. Passenger, vendor and payment fields were left out.

Aggregated results only

Everything is reported by borough, zone, hour or group. No individual trips or journeys are shown.

Cautious comparisons

Taxi, subway, bus and bike use different geographies, so comparisons are screening signals, not proof that one mode replaces another.

Careful wording

Zones are candidates for planning review, not final infrastructure decisions, and one month of data is flagged as a limit.
