The price was right. The framing needed work.

Hound Forward planned three tiers. We tested them against what 100 dog owners said they would pay, and what makes them willing to pay more.

Insight: vet visits average A$548–630 a year, and acute treatment can exceed A$3,000. Framing the box as preventive care, not a luxury, justifies the premium price.

Launch to one segment first

Instead of targeting every dog owner at once, we sequenced the segments to protect cash flow and learn fast.

First · Months 1–3Millennials, 28–45

Highest spend and fastest to try new subscription brands, so they prove the model and fund early growth. Reached through short video, creators and email.

Second · Once provenGen X, 45–60

Loyal, higher household spend but cautious. They enter once reviews and vet endorsements exist to reassure them.

Later · Small testsGen Z, 18–27

Fast-growing and social, but lower income. Used to amplify awareness through TikTok, not to drive early revenue.

A three-phase launch

Each phase has a clear goal and the metrics that decide when to move on.

Phase 1Validate
  • One hero box at $89
  • First 200 customers
  • Track CAC, retention, NPS
Phase 2Optimise
  • Refine box contents
  • Test Gen X
  • Add vet content
Phase 3Scale
  • Partners and creators
  • Annual plans
  • Bundle upgrades
