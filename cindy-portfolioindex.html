NYC Mobility Pipeline & Intervention Analysis

Python · SQL · DuckDB · GeoPandas
  • 16.8M+ records analysed
  • 73 zones classified
  • 98/100 student exemplar

Built a reproducible data pipeline across NYC taxi, subway, bus and Citi Bike data to answer a single question: which taxi zones actually need a targeted mobility intervention, and what kind? Sourced and audited six raw datasets, engineered time-period and trip-distance features, then used a four-signal percentile framework to classify 73 candidate zones — rather than just ranking zones by volume.

  • Manhattan drives 87% of all trips, but the reason varies block by block — short-trip congestion in some zones, late-night reliance in others.
  • Queens shows the strongest long-trip pattern (79%), driven almost entirely by JFK/LaGuardia airport travel.

Why it matters — Replaces "biggest volume = biggest priority" guesswork with a defensible, signal-based shortlist a transport planning team could actually act on with limited budget.

Melbourne Housing Opportunity Dashboard

Diabetes Risk Prediction

Python · Orange
  • 16,360 health records
  • 0.833 AUC
  • 1st ranked in cohort

An end-to-end ML classification workflow — EDA, cleaning, imputation, feature selection — benchmarking Logistic Regression, Decision Tree and SVM on patient health data. Five-fold Grid Search plus Information Gain narrowed the model to 10 key predictors of diabetes risk.

Why it matters — A model like this could flag at-risk patients before symptoms escalate, shifting care from reactive treatment to earlier, proactive screening.

Darling Daughters — Digital Marketing Audit

Business Analyst Intern · Semrush, Social Blade, BuzzSumo

A full digital marketing audit for Darling Daughters, a Melbourne & Geelong-based NDIS and aged-care provider, covering SEO, social media, Google Business Profile and website performance, to explain why strong credibility assets (a 2026 national industry award, 96.9% customer satisfaction) weren't converting into new enquiries.

  • 100% of organic search traffic was branded — the site was essentially invisible to anyone not already searching for the company by name.
  • Social captions were written in third person, making brand-owned content read like an outsourced directory listing.
  • 290 backlinks from 60 referring domains produced almost no authority — a link-quality problem, not a link-quantity one.

Why it matters — Turned a vague "why isn't marketing working" problem into a concrete, prioritised fix list across content, site structure and backlinks.

Full audit shared with the client's permission for portfolio purposes; findings and figures are as reported at the time of the audit.
